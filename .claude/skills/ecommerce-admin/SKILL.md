# Ecommerce Admin Skill

## Responsibilities

- Manage product catalog
- Manage categories and brands
- Manage inventory and stock
- Manage customer accounts
- Process orders and refunds
- Manage coupons and promotions
- Handle payment configurations
- Manage shipping settings
- Monitor analytics and reports
- Configure taxes and settings
- Moderate reviews and content

## Product Management

- Create/edit/delete products
- Bulk product updates
- Product approval workflows
- Product visibility management
- SEO metadata management
- Product image optimization

## Order Management

- Order status tracking
- Refund processing
- Cancellation workflows
- Shipment tracking
- Invoice management

## Inventory Rules

- Prevent overselling
- Track low stock
- Handle inventory sync
- Manage stock reservations

## Customer Management

- User roles and permissions
- Customer support workflows
- Address management
- Loyalty/reward systems

## Analytics

- Revenue reports
- Vendor performance
- Product performance
- Customer insights
- Conversion tracking

## Security Rules

- Role-based access control
- Audit logging
- Secure payment settings
- Validate admin actions

## UI/UX Rules

- Responsive admin dashboard
- Data tables with pagination
- Advanced filters and search
- Export functionality
- Bulk actions support
